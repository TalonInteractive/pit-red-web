Privacy Policy

Effective 30th April 2026

TL;DR — Too Long; Didn't Read

Below is an easy-to-read version of our Privacy Policy. Please read further down if you require the full detailed privacy policy.

We collect information across Roblox, Discord, and Google Services to manage the community, including:

  • Identity: Roblox and Discord IDs, usernames, and your age.
  • Roleplay Info: Character name, age, DOB, group rank, and department (e.g., Police, Fire, Council).
  • Activity: Time spent in sessions and participation logs.
  • Gameplay: In-game settings and saved outfits stored via Roblox DataStore.
  • Support Information: Name, email address and corrisponding emails.

How we use your data:

  • Purpose: Data is used to track engagement, manage roles, and maintain moderation/safety.
  • Storage: We use Roblox DataStore for gameplay and Google Sheets/Forms for internal databases and applications.
  • Support: We use your support information (your name, email and what you sent), so we can provide help and answer your inquiry.
  • No Sales: We do not sell your data.

Who can access your data, and how long we keep it for:

  • Access: Only authorised staff (Moderators, Game Owners, and Leadership) can view your data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Support: Our support email address is currently ran by the game owners only.
  • Retention: Data is kept while you are active. If you leave, it is typically deleted after 6 months of inactivity or immediately upon request.

If you live in the UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data:

  • Restriction: You can request that we limit how data is used, but this may prevent you from participating if the data is required for core community functions.
  • Requests: You can contact us at hello@pitlochryredefined.co.uk for any data requests.

Age requirements:

  • Our community is intended for users aged 15 and over. However, in compliance with Roblox's content maturity and publication standards, access to Pitlochry, Redefined is currently restricted to users aged 16 and over.
  • We will delete any data from anyone under 13 if discovered.

Updates:

  • Significant changes are announced in the Public Onboarding Discord server. Continued participation after updates counts as acceptance of the new policy.

This TL;DR is a simplification. Please refer to the complete policy below for detailed information.

1. Overview

Pitlochry, Redefined ("we", "our", "us") is a Roblox-based roleplay community supported by Discord servers and external tools. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By participating in our community, you agree to this policy.

2. Platforms We Use

We operate across the following services:

  • Discord — For communication and community servers
  • Roblox — For gameplay and data storage via DataStore
  • Google Sheets — Internal database and moderation logs
  • Google Forms — Applications and feedback
  • FormSubmit - API for contact form
  • Zoho Mail - Email service

Our Discord servers include:

  • Public Onboarding server
  • Operations server
  • SEPA server
  • Perth & Kinross Council server
  • Scottish Fire & Rescue Service server
  • Scottish Ambulance Service server
  • Police Scotland Perth and Kinross server

3. Data We Collect

Identification Data

  • Roblox User ID
  • Roblox Username
  • Roblox Age Group
  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Username

Used to identify users within our systems.

Roleplay Data

  • Character Name
  • Character Date of Birth
  • Character Age
  • Character Organisation
  • Character Job/Rank
  • Character Drivers Licence Validity

Used to manage your roleplay position and permissions.

Activity Data

Session participation (e.g. time spent in roleplay sessions) to track engagement, log hours for moderation and staff roles, and improve the experience.

Gameplay Data (Roblox DataStore)

  • Preferred settings
  • Saved outfits

Support Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message sent in form
  • Data sent throughout support ticket interaction

Used to provide support when you fill out the contact us form.

4. How We Store Data

We store data using:

  • Roblox DataStore (for in-game data)
  • Google Sheets (for logs, moderation, role tracking, and roleplay data)
  • Google Forms (for applications and feedback)
  • Zoho Mail (for support email)

We take reasonable steps to limit access and protect stored information.

5. How Long We Keep Data

Data is kept while you are a member of Pitlochry, Redefined.

If you leave:

  • Your data may be deleted upon request immediately
  • If no request is made, data is typically removed after 6 months of inactivity

Support data, such as: name, email address and emails that corrispond with your support ticket are deleted within 30 after the support ticket was closed.

6. Who Can Access Your Data

Access is restricted to authorised individuals only, including:

  • Senior members of emergency services divisions
  • Local authority leadership (e.g. council roles)
  • Game owners

Access is granted strictly on a need-to-know basis, for example:

  • Session hosts logging activity
  • Leadership managing roles or progression

Emails sent to our email addresses are seen by the game owners only.

7. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Operate and manage the roleplay community
  • Assign and manage roles/jobs
  • Track participation and activity
  • Support moderation and community safety
  • Provide help via emails

We do not sell your data.

8. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

If you are in the UK, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Rectification — request corrections to your data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Restriction — you can request that we limit how your data is used

In some cases, restricting processing may prevent us from continuing to provide access to the roleplay community, as certain data is required for core functionality (such as role assignment and activity tracking).

Where this applies, you may be removed from active participation while the restriction is in place. Your data will not be deleted unless you also request erasure.

9. Requesting Data or Deletion

You can request access, updates, or deletion of your data by contacting:

  • Email: hello@pitlochryredefined.co.uk

We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Children's Data

Our community is intended for users aged 15 and over. However, in compliance with Roblox's content maturity and publication standards, access to Pitlochry, Redefined is currently restricted to users aged 16 and over.

If we become aware that data has been collected from a user under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you believe this has occurred, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time.

Changes and updates will be communicated in the announcements channel within the "Pitlochry, Redefined. Public Onboarding" Discord server, and as a notification when you visit this website.

Continued participation in the community after any changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

The latest version of this policy will always be available on this website page (www.pitlochryredefined.co.uk/privacy).

12. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: hello@pitlochryredefined.co.uk
